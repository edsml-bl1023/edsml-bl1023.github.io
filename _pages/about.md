<section class="content-section" id="panel-research-experience" data-label="Research Experience" markdown="1">

## Research Experience {#research-experience}

From September 2026 to March 2027, I am visiting the **Department of the Geophysical Sciences, University of Chicago**, hosted by **Mingyi Wang**.

During my undergraduate studies, I worked on satellite-based precipitation retrieval under the supervision of **Prof. Emily Black** and **Dr. Ross Maidment** (University of Reading). At Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology (NUIST), I conducted research on tropical cyclones and precipitation under the supervision of **Prof. Ying Wu**.

At Imperial College London, under the supervision of **Dr. César Quilodrán-Casas** (Imperial College London) and **Dr. Laura Anne Mansfield** (Stanford University), I explored integrating machine learning with climate model outputs.

Currently, under the supervision of **Prof. Meng Gao** at HKBU, my PhD research investigates climate–aerosol interactions using climate models, with an emphasis on dust optical properties and radiative effects.
